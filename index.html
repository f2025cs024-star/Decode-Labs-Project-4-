Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from BSCS students at NIIT — click any question to expand.

Your Cumulative GPA (CGPA) is the weighted average of all semester GPAs. Multiply each semester's GPA by its total credit hours, sum them up, then divide by the total credit hours across all semesters. For example, if Semester 1 (15 cr) = 3.5 and Semester 2 (18 cr) = 3.8, then CGPA = (15×3.5 + 18×3.8) / (15+18) ≈ 3.66.

At NIIT, a minimum grade of D (1.0) is required to pass a course. However, a CGPA of at least 2.0 must be maintained to remain in good academic standing. Courses with an F grade (0.0) must be retaken, and the new grade replaces the old one in CGPA calculation.

Visit the Registrar's Office or submit a request through the student portal. Fill out the Transcript Request Form, pay the processing fee (typically PKR 500), and allow 3–5 working days for processing. Urgent requests may incur an additional fee. Digital transcripts can also be emailed directly to institutions upon request.

Popular elective tracks include Artificial Intelligence (Machine Learning, NLP, Computer Vision), Cybersecurity (Network Security, Ethical Hacking), and Web & Mobile Development (Full-Stack Development, React Native). Choose electives that align with your career goals. Consult your academic advisor before registration to ensure prerequisites are met.

Submit a Semester Freeze Application to the Registrar's Office before the add/drop deadline (usually the second week of the semester). Valid reasons include medical emergencies, financial hardship, or personal circumstances. A freeze preserves your current CGPA and does not count toward the maximum allowed semesters. You may freeze a maximum of two semesters during the entire program.

The FYP spans two semesters (FYP-I and FYP-II). In FYP-I, you form a team of 2–3 students, submit a project proposal, and complete the requirement analysis and design phase. FYP-II covers implementation, testing, and the final defense presentation before an evaluation panel. Regular supervisor meetings and milestone reviews are mandatory throughout.
